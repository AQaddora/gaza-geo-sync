# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone who builds software for people on
weak networks — wherever they live, whatever language they write in, whatever their level of
experience. We pledge to make participation free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive, specific feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use the software

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information (including location data) without explicit permission
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

Discussions here are about the software. Please keep them that way.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful.
Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub (open a minimal issue asking for a
private contact, without details, or use GitHub's built-in reporting). All reports are reviewed and kept confidential.
